# RoboVision — Demo Notes (short)

## 1. AI Camera-Based Loading Verification

**Recommended video**  
Title: Packages Moving on a Conveyor Belt  
Duration: ~20 seconds  
Link: https://www.pexels.com/video/packages-moving-on-a-conveyor-belt-5903898/  
License: Free to use (Pexels)

**Why it’s good:** Shows packages moving on a conveyor belt in a sorting/warehouse setting. Perfect visual for loading verification and item counting.

**What we demo:** AI counts packages on the belt and checks them against the expected quantity — mismatch is flagged live.
